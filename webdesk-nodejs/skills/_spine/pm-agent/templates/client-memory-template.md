## 1. About the Client

- Industry/niche, business stage, geographic markets, revenue tier (if known), key competitors, brand voice.
- Why they chose us (referral / repeat / specific capability).

## 2. Stakeholders

- **Primary contact:** name, role, decision authority, best channel, typical response time, timezone, communication style.
- **Technical contact:** who owns ERP/store credentials and access decisions.
- Secondary contacts + when to involve.

## 3. Systems & Integration Landscape (the load-bearing section for middleware)

- **ERP/CRM:** [system + version/edition]; API surface verified [Y/N]; **sandbox available?**; auth model; documented rate limits; on-prem vs cloud / VPN.
- **Store:** [BigCommerce/Shopify] + plan tier; webhook support used.
- **Entities synced + directions + system-of-record + conflict rules** (carry the approved contract summary).
- **Per-entity cron cadences** (in their timezone) and why.
- Quirks discovered: undocumented endpoints, rate-limit surprises, field-mapping gotchas, clock-skew, ordering issues.
- Credentials live in: [secure location reference — NOT the actual secrets].

## 4. Technical Preferences

- Stack choices they pay for / prefer (DB, host, queue). Tools they like / rejected and why.
- Data sensitivity posture; any compliance constraints (PII, region).

## 5. Tenancy & Dashboard Preferences

- per-client vs master; RBAC roles they actually use; modules they value; theme preferences.

## 6. Working Style

- PM tool/cadence; approval times; who approves contracts vs schema vs design; scope discipline (CR frequency, scope-creep tendency).

## 7. Past Projects History

Per project: name, dates, type, outcome, key learnings, estimation accuracy (estimate vs actual).

## 8. What Worked / What Didn't

Specific, repeatable patterns to continue; specific things to avoid next time (e.g. "showing the field mapping as raw JSON confused them — use a table; they sign off the mapping fast when it's a table").

## 9. Quirks & Preferences

Specific details that don't fit elsewhere but matter (launch-day preferences, who reviews what, hard "never do" rules).

## 10. Financial History

Typical budget range, pricing model, payment timing, net terms, currency; what they'll pay more for.

## 11. Open Threads at Close

Deferred-to-v2 items, promised-but-not-delivered, retainer/monitoring discussion, next-project conversation.

## 12. Recommended Approach Next Time

Start with / avoid / suggest-to-client — based on what we observed.

## 13. Files & Resources

Master doc, final spec, approved contracts/schema, verified ERP/store API doc references, brand assets, credentials location (reference only).
